Coachwork

A Shopify theme for car and performance parts stores. Shoppers choose their vehicle once and see only the parts that fit, with no app needed. This guide covers everything from installing the theme to setting up fitment data.

Getting started

  1. In your Shopify admin, go to Online Store → Themes, find Coachwork in the Theme Store and click Try theme or Add to theme library.
  2. Pick a preset: Coachwork (dark) or Alabaster (light). You can switch later under Theme settings → Colors.
  3. Click Customize to open the theme editor. Every section shows finished-looking placeholder content until you add your own images, products and collections.
  4. Add vehicle tags to your products (see Fitment data) so the vehicle finder has something to search.
  5. Preview the store, then click Publish.
Demo store images don't transfer when you install a theme. Sections show placeholders until you choose your own images in the theme editor.

Fitment data

Coachwork reads vehicle fitment from product tags. Add one tag per vehicle a product fits, in this format:

ymm:YEAR|make|model

For example, a brake kit that fits two cars gets two tags:

ymm:2024|aurelia|grand-tourer
ymm:2023|aurelia|saloon
  • Write the make and model in lowercase, with hyphens instead of spaces. The theme shows them capitalized: grand-tourer appears as "Grand Tourer".
  • Add tags in Products → [product] → Tags, or in bulk with a product CSV import (the Tags column, comma-separated).
  • Products without ymm: tags are treated as universal: they don't show a fit badge and the fitment check stays hidden.
The vehicle finder reads up to 1,000 tags across your store (a Shopify limit on store-wide tags). For very large catalogs, add a fitment app: the product page and collection grid have app blocks where a fitment app can take over.

Vehicle finder and My Garage

The vehicle finder is a block in the Slideshow section. It lists every year found in your ymm: tags, then the makes for that year, then the models.

  • Find parts opens the collection filtered to that exact vehicle, for example /collections/all/ymm-2024-aurelia-grand-tourer. Choose which collection in the block's Collection setting.
  • Save to My Garage stores the vehicle in the shopper's browser (up to 5 vehicles). No account or app is needed.
  • With a saved vehicle, product cards show a fit badge, product pages confirm the fit, and the featured collection heading names the vehicle.
  • Shoppers open My Garage from the header to switch vehicles, shop for one or remove it.

Anatomy of fit

A large photo with numbered points, each linked to a product. Shoppers select a point to see the part, its part number and price.

  1. Add the Anatomy of fit section and choose an image, ideally a car photographed side-on or close up.
  2. Add up to 8 Hotspot blocks. For each one choose a product, a label (for example "Headlight") and a zone (for example "Lighting").
  3. Set the horizontal and vertical position as a percentage of the image. 0, 0 is the top-left corner.

Points are buttons, so they work with the keyboard and screen readers. The list under the image gives the same parts in text.

Header and footer

  • Logo: upload it under Theme settings → Logo and favicon and set its width. Without a logo, the store name is shown in the heading font.
  • Menus: the header shows up to three menu levels. Top-level items with child links open a mega menu panel, with the second level as columns. Add a Mega menu promo block for an image card in the panel.
  • Announcement bar: add messages as blocks; they scroll and can be paused.
  • Footer: add Email signup, Menu, Store information and Social media icons blocks. The payment icons and Shopify credit are added automatically.
  • Country and language selectors appear when you sell in more than one market or language.

Product pages

Every element on the product page is a block you can reorder or remove: vendor, title, part number (the variant SKU), price, inventory status, variant picker, quantity, buy buttons, Shop Pay Installments, purchase options, pickup availability, fitment check, description, collapsible tabs, complementary products, share and Custom Liquid.

  • Fitment check confirms whether the part fits the shopper's saved vehicle and lists every compatible vehicle from the product's tags.
  • Swatches: add swatches to your product options in the admin and the variant picker shows them automatically.
  • Media: images, videos, YouTube or Vimeo, and 3D models with AR, with a full-screen zoom.

Collections and search

  • Filters come from Search & Discovery in your admin. The theme shows them in a sidebar on desktop and a drawer on mobile, including price range and color swatches.
  • Shoppers can sort and switch between grid and list view. Filters update the page without a reload.
  • Predictive search suggests products, collections and pages as shoppers type.

Cart

Choose a drawer or page cart under Theme settings → Cart. The cart supports discount codes, order notes, unit prices, subscriptions and accelerated checkout buttons.

FAQ

Do I need an app for the vehicle finder?

No. The finder, My Garage, fit badges and the fitment check all read your products' ymm: tags. For catalogs with more than 1,000 vehicle tags, a fitment app can plug into the theme's app blocks.

Why doesn't a year, make or model appear in the finder?
  • Check the tag format: ymm:2024|aurelia|grand-tourer, with no spaces.
  • The product must be active and available on the Online Store.
  • The finder reads the first 1,000 tags in your store. If you have more, remove unused tags or use a fitment app.
Where is a shopper's garage saved?

In their browser. It stays on that device and isn't linked to a customer account, so no personal data is stored.

Why do my sections show placeholder images after installing?

Demo store images don't transfer with the theme. Choose your own images in each section in the theme editor.

How do I change the hotspot positions in Anatomy of fit?

Open the hotspot block and change its horizontal and vertical position, as percentages of the image. The editor preview updates as you move the sliders.

Can I use Coachwork for motorcycle parts or other vehicles?

Yes. The tags work for any vehicle with a year, make and model. Change the copy and images to suit your store.

How do I show a different hero image on phones?

Each slide has a Mobile image setting. Phones download only the mobile image.

Does the theme support multiple languages and currencies?

Yes. Add languages and markets in your admin, and the header shows language and country selectors.

How do I update to a new version?

When an update is available, Shopify shows it in Online Store → Themes. Updating creates a copy with your settings carried over. See the changelog for what changed.
