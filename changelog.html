1.0.0

First release.

  • Vehicle finder and My Garage: year, make and model search from ymm: product tags, up to five saved vehicles, fit badges on product cards, and a fitment check with compatible vehicles on product pages.
  • Anatomy of fit: products pinned to a photo with keyboard-accessible hotspots.
  • Header: centered logo, three-level mega menu with a promo block, predictive search, customer account menu, country and language selectors, sticky option.
  • Home page: slideshow with mobile images, collection list, featured collection, brand index, image with text, trust strip, testimonials, video and more.
  • Product pages: every element is a block; images, video and 3D media with zoom; swatches; pickup availability; gift card recipient form.
  • Collections and search: filters with price range and swatches, sorting, grid or list view.
  • Cart: drawer or page, discounts, notes and accelerated checkout.
  • Presets: Coachwork (dark) and Alabaster (light).
